# Mantus landing-page art

These are original project assets generated for the public website. They are
not copied from Tibia or another game. All three use the established Mantus
palette: near-black forged iron, cold slate, muted pewter, oxblood accents,
and tightly controlled cyan or ember light.

## `mantus-hero.webp`

Wide painterly key art of a lone armored traveler crossing a rain-dark stone
causeway toward a mountain fortress under a cold moon. The traveler and gate
sit on the right, leaving calm negative space for the page copy on the left.
No text, logos, UI, modern objects, or recognizable third-party locations.

## `astral-vault.webp`

Landscape painterly editorial art of three anonymous adventurers discovering
an ancient underground ritual chamber. A restrained cyan seal and dim red
braziers light weathered stone, broken statues, and corroded iron. No text,
logos, UI, or recognizable third-party characters.

## `frontier-road.webp`

Landscape painterly editorial art of four anonymous adventurers following a
rain-soaked forest road toward a distant watchtower. The high three-quarter
view lightly recalls classic top-down exploration without copying any game
asset or location. No text, logos, UI, or copyrighted symbols.

The built-in image-generation workflow produced PNG sources; the checked-in
WebP files were encoded at quality 84 for the website.
